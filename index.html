14 Describing your week

You can talk about your usual weekly activities with the present simple and time phrases. Tap 🔊 or a card to hear English.

14.1 Days of the week

Tap a day to hear it and see the French.

Saturday + Sunday = the weekend (le week-end).

14.2 Prepositions and days

I go to work on Mondays.

Use on before the day. Add -s for “every Monday”.

I work from Monday to Friday.

from = start day · to = finish day

I watch TV on / at the weekend.
“On the weekend” is more common in the US. “At the weekend” is more common in the UK. In US English: “I work Mondays.”
